Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026 · App: Jetfall · Bundle ID: com.jetfallair.app

Summary

Jetfall is an offline game. It does not require an account, does not show ads, does not include in-app purchases, and does not use tracking frameworks.

Data stored on your device

Progress and preferences may be saved locally on your device, including:

  • campaign / mission progress
  • credit balance and owned aircraft
  • settings such as sound and crosshair preferences
  • local high scores / best runs

This information stays on your device unless you delete the app or clear its data.

Data we do not collect

  • name, email, or contact details
  • precise or approximate location
  • advertising identifiers for tracking
  • analytics or crash reports to third parties
  • photos, contacts, microphone, or camera content

Internet access

The current version is designed to work offline and does not require a network connection to play.

Children

The game does not target children specifically and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes

If the app later adds online features, analytics, or advertising, this policy will be updated and the App Store privacy labels will be revised before those features ship.
